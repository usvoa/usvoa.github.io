# U.S. Virtual Observatory Alliance

The U.S. Virtual Observatory Alliance, or USVOA, is the U.S. member organization in the International Virtual Observatory Alliance.
USVOA does not currently operate a public mailing list, help desk, or user-support program.
This page is provided to direct visitors to current Virtual Observatory resources and to U.S. institutions that are active in Virtual Observatory-related work.

## International Virtual Observatory Alliance

The International Virtual Observatory Alliance, or IVOA, develops and maintains the international standards that enable interoperable discovery, access, and use of astronomical data and services.

Use IVOA for current standards, working groups, meetings, recommendations, and international coordination.

IVOA website: https://www.ivoa.net/
